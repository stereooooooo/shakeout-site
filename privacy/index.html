Privacy policy

Effective 10 October 2026

Dewline collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no Dewline server. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer.

Your location

Dewline asks for your location only while you're using it. Your location is used on your iPhone, and sent off it only for these:

  • To Apple, for the forecast. Dewline gets its weather from Apple Weather (WeatherKit), which needs the place you want the forecast for.
  • To Apple, for the place name. Dewline asks Apple Maps for the name of the place, such as the town you're in.
  • To the US National Weather Service, for alert bulletins. When Apple Weather reports a National Weather Service alert for the place on screen, whether that's where you are or a place you're viewing, Dewline asks the NWS (api.weather.gov) for that alert's bulletin, sending that place's location rounded to about 100 metres. No account or key is involved, and nothing else is sent.

When you look up a place with Find, what you type is sent to Apple Maps to find matches. A place you look up is kept only if you save it.

Apple handles these requests under Apple's privacy policy, and the National Weather Service under its own.

What stays on your iPhone

Your paces, scheduled runs and units, the places you save, the most recent forecast, and your last known location (so the widgets can show the forecast) are stored on your iPhone, in storage shared only between the Dewline app and its widgets. They aren't sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

Dewline collects no data from anyone, children included.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics, and its fonts are served from the site itself. GitHub may log visits as described in GitHub's privacy statement.
