Support

Found a bug, or something that reads wrong? Open an issue on GitHub.

Open an issue

You'll need a free GitHub account. Issues are public, so don't include your exact location, your email address or anything else you'd rather keep private.

What helps

  • What you saw, and what you expected.
  • The screen it was on, and a screenshot if you can.
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • Roughly where and when: a town and an hour is plenty.

Before you write

No forecast, or "Shakeout can't see where you are." Shakeout needs location access while you're using it: Settings › Apps › Shakeout › Location › While Using the App. Or use Find to look up a place.

A widget says "Open Shakeout to refresh". The widget has run through its saved forecast. Opening the app fetches a new one. iOS decides how often widgets refresh, so they can lag a little.
